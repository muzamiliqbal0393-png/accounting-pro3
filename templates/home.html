About ACCOUNTING PRO

A complete computerized accounting system, built the way accountants work

ACCOUNTING PRO is a full double-entry accounting and business management system for small and medium businesses. It replaces manual cash books and spreadsheets with one controlled set of records: you post vouchers, and every report — trial balance, trading account, profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow — is produced from those same entries.

The system follows standard accounting practice. Opening balances are entered per account, posted vouchers can never be edited or deleted (only reversed), adjusting entries are kept separate from ordinary vouchers, and closing entries move the period result into capital. Money is stored in exact decimal values, never floating point, so totals always reconcile.

Every business gets its own isolated data. Nothing is shared, nothing is simulated, and no report ever forces a balancing figure — if your books do not balance, the report says so.
