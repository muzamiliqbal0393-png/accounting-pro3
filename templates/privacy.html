Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ today.strftime('%d %B %Y') }}

ACCOUNTING PRO is an accounting application. This policy explains what the system stores and how it is used.

1. Your accounting data

Everything you enter — business profile, accounts, vouchers, adjustments, products, inventory, budgets and imports — is stored in the application's own database and belongs to you. Data is isolated per user account: no other account can read, list or report on your business records.

2. Account information

Registration stores your name, email address and a password hash. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 and are never stored or displayed in readable form. Your email address is used only to sign you in and to identify your account.

3. Contact messages

If you use the contact form on the home page, the name, email address, subject and message you submit are recorded in the application's contact log so the message can be answered. They are not used for marketing and are not shared with third parties.

4. Cookies and sessions

The application uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. A CSRF token is kept in the session to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.

5. Files you upload

Logos, voucher attachments and imported spreadsheets are stored in the application's upload folder and are limited by type and size (maximum 8 MB per file). Database backups you create are stored in the same private area until you download or delete them.

6. Your control over the data

  • Download a complete backup of your database at any time from Backup & Restore.
  • Export every report as PDF, Word or Excel.
  • Review who did what and when in the audit log.
  • Remove your own records through the application's normal, auditable actions.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form on the home page.
