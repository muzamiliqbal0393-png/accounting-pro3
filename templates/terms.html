2. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your login credentials confidential and use your own account.

3. Accounting integrity rules

Posted vouchers cannot be edited or deleted; corrections are made by posting a reversal or an adjusting entry, which keeps the audit trail complete. Reports never insert a balancing figure: if your books do not balance, the report shows the difference so it can be fixed.
