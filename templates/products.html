How inventory links to accounting

Recording a sale or purchase in the Inventory module can post a matching journal draft:

  • Sale: Dr Cash / Receivable, Cr Sales
  • Purchase: Dr Purchases, Cr Cash / Payable

Inventory quantities are only treated as accounting facts when supported by posted vouchers or your confirmed opening stock.
